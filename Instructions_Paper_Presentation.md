# Instructions for Paper Presentation

Each student will have to present one paper in class in front of their classmates. This will contribute 35% of your overall grade. This paper must be related to you research proposal. In fact, I want you to pick a paper that can be classified as one of the following:

1. The paper that is the closest one to what you will write on your research proposal. For example, if you are extending a model from a given paper, you should present that paper. If you are applying a certain technique to a different context, you should present the paper that originally developed that technique.
2. The seminal paper of the literature you are working on. For example, if you are interested on a certain empirical puzzle, you should present the first paper that documented that first documented that puzzle.



This presentation has many goals:

- You will help me gain context about what you are doing and why it matters. It will help me better understand your research proposal.
- You will give context to your classmates about what you are interested. It will help them peer-review your proposal better.
- You will practice your presentation skills. This is very important for your future career as a researcher. Remember: *we are in the business of selling ideas*.

## Check-in
As described in the [Instructions for Research Proposal](Instructions_Research_Proposal.md), you need to email me a two-paragraph summary of your research idea by the end of the week containing lecture 10. In that email, you also need to include the paper you plan to present and should defend why that paper fits in one of the two categories above. I will give you feedback on whether that paper is a good fit or not.

## Format and Grading

We will decide regarding length after we know how many students are in the class. Your grade, out of 100 points, will be based on the following criteria:

- The delivery of presentation, i.e., how engaging and clear you are (50 points)
- The quality of your slides (35 points). Please minimize text and do things professionally. You might not like it, but looks do matter.
- How you handle questions from me and your classmates (15 points).
